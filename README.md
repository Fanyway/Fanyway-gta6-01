**SOLMAR — Streets of Solmar Bay.** An original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built from scratch in three.js. You are Dario Voss, fresh off the night bus in a sun-soaked coastal city run by fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police, take five story jobs — and make the bay yours.

- **A living city:** a 480 m road grid with downtown towers, suburbs, a park, a beach and an ocean, traffic that drives the roads, pedestrians who stroll the sidewalks and flee when shots ring out.
- **Cars to steal and drive:** arcade driving with handbrake slides, traffic to weave through, and a gold Aurelio GT to steal in the finale.
- **Wanted level:** 0–5 stars. Police cars chase and ram you, officers on foot try to arrest you, and the heat fades when you stay clean.
- **Missions and a story:** five jobs from Mara Quinn — car theft, a timed delivery, losing the heat, a warehouse shootout, and the big score — plus twelve hidden cash stashes.
- **Combat, HUD and save:** a pistol with tracers and muzzle flashes, a live minimap, health/armor/money/wanted display, a full day cycle, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, and autosave to localStorage.

Everything is original — the city, characters, vehicles, story, art and sounds are invented for this game, nothing is taken from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Keyboard + mouse: WASD to move and drive, mouse to look, E to enter a car or start a job, left click to shoot, Shift to sprint, Space to jump or handbrake, Esc to pause.
