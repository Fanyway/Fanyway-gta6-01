SOLMAR
Streets of Solmar Bay

A sun-soaked open-world crime story. You are Dario Voss, fresh off the night bus with empty pockets and a phone number for Mara Quinn — the fixer who runs this city from a garage on Harbor Row. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay Police, take her jobs, and make the bay yours.

On foot: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, mouse to look, left click to shoot your pistol.
In a car: W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space is the handbrake, E gets you out.
Steal cars: walk up to any car and press E. If someone sees you, the police take notice.
Wanted level: crimes raise your stars. Police cars chase and ram you; officers on foot try to arrest you. Stay clean and out of sight and the heat fades.
Missions: drive to Mara's Garage and press E to start a job. Finish all five to own the bay. Twelve cash stashes are hidden around the city — $100 each.
